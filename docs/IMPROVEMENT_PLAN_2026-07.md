## 0. State of the project (honest baseline)

This is a genuinely strong project. The federal pipeline has real incremental-fetch engineering (checkpoints, cycle chunking, supersession instead of clobbering), the three-tier classification is enforced in code, snapshot-before-mutation and provenance logging actually happen, the two published legislation briefs re-verify numerically to the dollar, and the site's provenance drawers, state-coverage funnel page, and status-chip discipline are better than most professional data journalism. The review found **no fabricated data, no orphaned rows, no dangling supersessions, and zero spec violations in the stored status distribution** (3,941 CONFIRMED / $33.93M · 213 PROBABLE / $1.09M federal; ~$15M state across 10 jurisdictions).

The problems cluster into five themes:

1. **The headline numbers are overstated by known, fixable mechanisms** — earmark/conduit double-counting federally (~$961K exposure) and cross-filing duplicates in IL/AZ/TX state data (up to ~$2.4M). For an archive whose product is citable numbers, these are the top priority of everything in this document.
2. **Provenance is structurally broken for automation-era rows** — raw payloads fetched by the CI cron are destroyed with the runner; `GOVERNANCE.md` §1.4's "reconstructible from raw" guarantee is currently false for anything the monthly refresh ingested.
3. **Failures are silent.** `continue-on-error` keeps refresh jobs green when owners fail; no notification exists anywhere; a state source can skip for months invisibly (MN did exactly this).
4. **What shipped and what didn't has drifted from belief.** Household tracking (PR #27) is still open, not live; the CSV exports are gitignored and a month stale; the methodology page still says state data is out of scope; DONATION_SCHEMA.md documents 4 of master.db's 11 tables.
5. **The next research frontier is known and mapped** — the household layer (Randy Kendrick ~$2M AZ alone), the thin-owner watchlist, the 10,106-item state review queue with no adjudication tooling, and a legislation layer that can currently join only ~1% of archive dollars to legislators.

Standing user directives honored throughout: **refactors are on HOLD** (documented in §8, not scheduled); **spouse/household expansion was deferred by the user** (presented as a decision point, not a task); LFS setup stays as-is.

### 1.1 Federal earmark/conduit double-counting (M) — ✅ PR #65
Found independently by two review passes. The Schedule A fetch (`scripts/fetch_fec.py:336-346`) never requests/stores `memo_code` or `is_individual`; the `donations` table has no memo column. An earmarked gift via ActBlue/WinRed appears twice — conduit receipt + memo entry at the ultimate recipient — under distinct `sub_id`s, so both classify CONFIRMED and both sum. Verified in master.db: **152 same-donor/same-day/same-amount conduit↔recipient pairs; 151 conduit rows ≈ $961K**; `receipt_type_full` shows dozens of literal "EARMARKED FOR/THROUGH …" rows. Joint-fundraising-committee transfer memos are the same mechanic. FEC's own guidance excludes memo entries from totals.
**Fix:** add `memo_code`, `memo_text`, `is_individual` columns (schema v8); backfill from stored raw where available; exclude memo rows from every `SUM` (export.py, build_data.py, by_household); show both legs in the drawer with an "earmarked via …" badge.
**Acceptance:** the 152 pairs collapse to single counted legs; dashboard/README totals restated; a regression test with a synthetic conduit pair.

> **2026-07-10 execution note (§1.1 — ✅ PR #65):** Verified against local raw (the 4.8GB IS on this machine). The distinguishing field is FEC's **`is_individual`**, NOT `memo_code` — `memo_code` is `None` on both conduit and recipient legs of ActBlue/WinRed earmarks, and is set on 982 rows most of which are the *countable* recipient legs (so excluding on `memo_code` would be wrong). The plan's "$961K / 151 conduit rows" **overcounts the true exposure**: 62 of the 170 `is_individual=False` rows are **lone** conduit legs with no paired countable sibling ($497,700 of real giving — nearly all of Ken Kendrick's WinRed-earmarked donations); excluding all conduit legs would erase them. The FEC-correct, safe rule implemented: a conduit/passthrough leg (`is_individual=False`) is excluded **only when a countable sibling leg exists** in the same (owner, donor, date, amount) group. That excludes **108 rows / $683,135.35** (101 CONFIRMED / $666,535.35 + 7 PROBABLE / $16,600). Represented as a derived `counted` flag (schema v8), recomputed at ingest/reclassify; all published SUM surfaces filter `counted = 1`. The excluded conduit leg drops from the counted view; the more-informative recipient leg (which already carries the "EARMARKED" memo text) remains. The "both legs with badge" drawer nicety was deferred in favor of the lower-risk single-filter-point approach.

### 1.3 Persist `sub_id`; fix the dedup-key hierarchy (M) — ✅ PR #70
